Returns & Refund Policy

Customer satisfaction is our utmost priority. We ensure complete transparency regarding returns, replacements, and refunds.

7-Day Easy Returns (Unstitched Items)

Unstitched silk half sarees, lehenga fabrics, and accessories can be returned or exchanged within 7 days of delivery provided they are unused, unwashed, with original tags intact.

Custom Stitched & Tailored Orders

Outfits that have been custom stitched or altered according to personal measurements cannot be returned for a cash refund. However, we offer Free Fit Alterations and doorstep pickup for adjustment within 10 days.

Refund Processing

  • Prepaid orders: Refund will be credited back to your original payment method (Bank/UPI/Card) within 5 to 7 business days after product inspection.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) orders: Refund will be issued via Store Credit Voucher or direct UPI transfer.
