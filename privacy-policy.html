Privacy Policy

Amzira values your trust and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal data is collected, used, and safeguarded.

All online transactions are encrypted via 256-bit SSL encryption. We do not store your credit card numbers or banking passwords on our servers.
