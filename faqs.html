Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about custom stitching, authentic Kanchipuram silks, and order delivery.

Custom Stitching & Tailoring

Do you offer custom blouse and lehenga tailoring?

Yes! We offer standard stitched sizes (S to XXL), semi-stitched, and 100% custom-tailored fitting based on your specific measurements (bust, shoulder, waist, and skirt length).

How do I send my measurements after placing an order?

You can enter your measurements directly on the product detail page before adding to cart, or submit them through your account dashboard or via WhatsApp with our VIP tailoring specialist.

Authenticity & Fabric Care

Are your Kanjeevaram Lehengas Silk Mark Certified?

Absolutely. All our pure Kanjeevaram and Kanchipuram silk products carry official Silk Mark certification guaranteeing 100% pure mulberry silk and authentic zari weaving.

How should I store and clean my silk lehenga?

We strongly recommend Dry Clean Only. Store your silk half sarees and lehengas wrapped in clean cotton cloth in a cool, dry place to maintain the lustre of the zari border.

Shipping & Returns

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship worldwide to USA, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, Malaysia, Singapore, and 50+ countries with DHL and FedEx Express.

What is your return policy for custom-stitched items?

Unstitched products can be returned within 7 days. Custom-tailored garments are made specially for your measurements, so we offer free alterations if any fitting adjustments are required.
