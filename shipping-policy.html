Shipping & Delivery Policy

At Amzira, we ensure your wedding and festive wear reaches you safely and on time, anywhere in India and across the globe.

Domestic Shipping (India)

  • Free Express Shipping: Available on all domestic orders above ₹2,000.
  • Standard Delivery Timeline: 4 to 7 business days for unstitched outfits.
  • Custom Tailoring Orders: Additional 5 to 7 business days for stitching & quality assurance.

International Shipping

We ship to USA, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, Singapore, Malaysia, and 50+ countries via courier partners (DHL, FedEx, Blue Dart).

  • International Express Delivery: 7 to 10 business days worldwide.
  • Customs & Import Duties: Import duties or local taxes, if applicable in destination countries, are borne by the recipient.

Order Tracking

Once dispatched, a tracking ID and tracking link will be sent to your registered email and WhatsApp number. You can also track your shipment live on our Track Order Page.
